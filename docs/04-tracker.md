# Step 4 · Your tracker

In the app: **4. Your tracker**.

## The numbers at the top

- **Open now**: not closed or passed, and the deadline has not gone by.
- **Closing in 14 days**: apply to these first.
- **Strong fits**: scoring at or above the *strong* mark in your profile (default 75).
- **Sponsorship stated**: postings that explicitly say they sponsor visas.

## Filters and sorting

| Show | What you see |
|---|---|
| Open | Everything still open (the default) |
| Strong fits | Open and scoring at or above *strong* |
| Closing soon | Open, with a deadline in the next 21 days |
| Teaching | Teaching Professor, Lecturer and Instructor posts |
| Everything | Including closed and passed posts |

**Visa stated only** hides everything except postings that say they sponsor.

Sort by **deadline** (soonest first; no deadline goes last), **score**, or **institution**.

## The table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| score | Fit out of 100 ([how it is worked out](03-search.md#how-scoring-works)) |
| type | Which of the four role types it is |
| visa | ✅ yes · ⚠️ limited · ❔ unknown ([what these mean](03-search.md#how-visa-sponsorship-is-read)) |
| deadline / days left | Blank means none was found. Check the posting; many say "open until filled" |
| status | **Click to change**: watching, drafting, submitted, interview, offer, closed, passed |
| notes | **Click to type.** Starts with why it matched, plus any visa warning |
| link | Opens the posting |

Positions marked *closed*, *passed* or *offer* move out of the **Open** view.

## Keep your work

The app does not save anything. At the bottom of the page:

- **positions.json**: your full list with your statuses and notes. **Download this every
  time.** Upload it on step 1 next time and searches will skip what you already have.
- **Spreadsheet (CSV)**: opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Tracker page (HTML)**: a single page you can open in any browser, offline, and
  share. Status changes made on that page are saved in that browser.

**Clear all positions** empties the list in this session. Your downloaded files are not
affected.

## When a search finds the same job twice

The same posting is sometimes listed on two boards under slightly different employer
names, such as "University of Colorado Boulder" and "University of Colorado System".
Set the extra one to *passed*.
