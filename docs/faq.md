## Quick guide

1. **Make your profile** (step 2). Fill in the form, or paste the prompt into any free
   chatbot with your CV. Check *What the search will use*, then download `profile.md`.
2. **Search** (step 3). Click *Search now* and wait about 15 seconds.
3. **Track** (step 4). Change *status* and *notes* in the table. Download
   `positions.json` before you leave.
4. **Next time.** Upload `profile.md` and `positions.json` on step 1, then search
   again. Only new postings are added.

## Common questions

**Do I need an API key or a paid AI account?**
No. The search uses the job boards' free public feeds, and the scoring is a set of
written rules. AI is only an optional way to write the profile, and a free chatbot
works for that.

**Which jobs does it look for?**
Only Assistant Professor (including tenure-track and open rank), Teaching Professor
(including Lecturer and Instructor), Research Faculty (Research Assistant Professor,
Research Professor) and Research Scientist (including Project and Staff Scientist).
Postdocs, adjunct, visiting, administrative, and associate- or full-only posts are
ignored.

**How does it know about visa sponsorship?**
It reads each posting for phrases like "will not sponsor", "must be a US citizen" or
"visa sponsorship is available". If you need sponsorship, refusals are dropped.
Postings that say nothing are kept and marked *unknown*: ask the department.

**Why do most results say "unknown" for visa?**
Most university adverts do not mention visas at all. US universities are exempt
from the H-1B cap and sponsor often, so *unknown* is usually fine. Confirm it before
you spend a day on an application.

**Why does a job have no deadline?**
Many say "open until filled" or put the date in a format the search does not
recognise. Open the link and check.

**It missed a job I know about.**
It searches six boards: Chronicle, Inside Higher Ed, Nature Careers, Science Careers,
Times Higher Education and AGU. It does not search HigherEdJobs, AcademicJobsOnline,
LinkedIn or university HR sites. Also check whether the posting uses one of your core
keywords. If not, add the word the posting uses.

**Too many irrelevant results.**
Raise the threshold, make core keywords more specific (`crop remote sensing` rather
than `remote sensing`), or add avoid keywords.

**Too few results.**
Add more core keywords, lower the threshold, or set Locations to `anywhere`.

**Is my CV safe?**
The app reads your CV in memory to suggest keywords and never saves it. Nothing you
upload is stored on the server or visible to other visitors. When the tab closes, it
is gone, which is why you download your files.
