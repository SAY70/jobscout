Postdoctoral Research Associate, University of Washington, 2023 - present.
   Deploying and calibrating low-cost particulate matter sensor networks across Seattle
   and Tacoma. Built the calibration pipeline that corrects for humidity and sensor drift.
   Developing machine learning models that downscale satellite aerosol optical depth to
   neighbourhood-scale PM2.5 estimates, with uncertainty reported per grid cell.
   Co-designed a community science programme placing sensors with eight neighbourhood
   associations; co-authored the data-sharing agreement with city partners.

Graduate Research Assistant, University of Michigan, 2018 - 2023.
   Designed an enclosure and data logger for outdoor low-cost sensors; field tested in
   Lagos and Accra across two rainy seasons. Published the reference correction method
   now used by three other groups.
